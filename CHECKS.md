## Observation: stale page

**Question:** "What is the xiaomi mimo model mentioned in the course setup?"

**What happened:** Retrieval returned chunks from both `weeks/01/setup.md` (older page, lists `xiaomi/mimo-v2.5` as the instructor fallback) and `weeks/03/lab.md` (newer page, lists `xiaomi/mimo-v2.6-flash` as the comparison model for the eval). The model cited both pages and noted the version difference. The stale-page risk here is that a query could just as easily retrieve only the older chunk and surface the deprecated `v2.5` identifier — the corpus does not, on its own, prefer the newer page.

**Theoretical fix (no code change):** each chunk would carry a `last_modified` date in its metadata (from the source file's mtime at index time, or from a recorded last-edit date). In `_retrieve`, when two chunks' cosine scores are within a small margin (e.g., 0.05), the chunk with the newer `last_modified` would win. No change to the embedding model or the chunk text is required — only an extra metadata field per chunk and a tie-breaker in the ranking step.
